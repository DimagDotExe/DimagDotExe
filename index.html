01 / THE IDEA Curiosity, with a scientific lens.

The everyday
is more interesting
than it looks.

We spend our lives thinking, remembering, deciding, worrying, learning and reacting. Much of it feels ordinary—until we stop and ask why.

DIMAG.exe turns those familiar moments into questions worth exploring. We start with an experience people recognise, look at what psychology and science can tell us, and explain the ideas in a way that makes sense outside a textbook.

The aim isn't to diagnose you, sell miracle answers or reduce human behaviour to one catchy theory. It's to make good explanations easier to understand—and to make curiosity a habit.

Everyday experience is the starting point. Evidence is the guide. Understanding is the goal.

04 / GOOD TO KNOW A few useful answers.

Curiosity first.
Clarity always.

What exactly is DIMAG.exe?+

DIMAG.exe is an educational media project exploring psychology, brain science and human behaviour through everyday questions and accessible storytelling.

Which language are the videos in?+

The content is designed primarily in Hindi, with relevant English scientific terms explained clearly where they help understanding.

Where can I watch the videos?+

Visit the official YouTube channel, Instagram profile or Facebook page using the links in the Watch & Follow section. Content availability depends on what has been published on each platform.

How does DIMAG.exe approach science?+

The goal is to explain ideas accurately and in context. Scientific findings can have limitations, and educational content should not be treated as personal medical or mental-health advice.

Can I suggest a topic or collaborate?+

Yes. Use the contact section below when the email address is configured, or reach out through one of the official social profiles.
