Curious? Good.

What is DIMAG.exe?+

DIMAG.exe is an educational content project exploring psychology, brain science and human behaviour through everyday questions and research-led explanations.

What language will the videos use?+

The videos are planned primarily in Hindi, with relevant English scientific terms explained in context.

What kind of content can I expect?+

Short educational videos using narration, illustrations, animation and motion graphics to explain ideas from psychology and science.

Is the content already live?+

This website is the starting point. Content availability will be announced on the official social channels linked above.

How can I get in touch?+

A dedicated contact email will be added here once it is ready. For now, you can reach DIMAG.exe through its linked social profiles.
